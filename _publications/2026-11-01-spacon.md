**SpaCon** addresses semi-supervised 3D medical image segmentation under scarce annotation. The framework builds on the observation that conventional masked autoencoders can disrupt anatomical boundaries when masking image patches. Instead of discarding the unseen regions, SpaCon treats them as a supervision signal for the visible areas through a **complementary dual-mask mechanism** with **smooth boundary regularization**, allowing visible and masked pixels to learn bidirectionally.

Evaluated on **ACDC**, **LA**, and **Pancreas CT** with only **5–10% labeled data**, SpaCon outperforms competitive semi-supervised baselines in Dice Similarity Coefficient. The work was accepted to **PRICAI 2026** as an **Oral presentation (long paper track)**.
